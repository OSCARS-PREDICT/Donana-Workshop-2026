## The programme

**Is this a conference?**
No. There are very few presentations and a lot of working sessions. So come ready to work rather than to just listen.
You will be asked to bring your (v1 functional) code, progress it within the LifeWatch European Research Infrastructure Consortium (LWE ERIC or just LWE), get professional technical help in doing so (thanks LWE!), while bringing life to the PREDICT project. Be prepared to put your name against actions with dates to be held accountable. 

**Why is so much of it outdoors?**
The walking sessions will be optional/weather dependant (as we may just be into the rainy season here), but they are real working sessions. 
We aim for this informal time to be where collaboration actually gets agreed. Making connections with one another, while immersed in one of our case study ecosystems, we hope, will be a nice project memory and motivator to push things forward.

**Do I have to go birdwatching?**
No. The dawn walks and bird watching sessions sit outside the working programme and are optional. They should be good though as October is migration season here :). Expect cranes, waders, and plenty of other wildfowl.

**Is any of it hybrid?**
No, not really. The exception of one session on Day 2 featuring Emily joining online for a co-presentation with Billur who will be in the room, then logging off. The rest is in person only. 
We do not have confidence in the AV setup or the facilitation capacity for three days of good hybrid, and half-hearted hybrid is worse than none! 
All notes will be collated, synthesised and shared among the group so actions and outcomes are clear.

**What language will it be in?**
English. Most of us are working in a second or third language. Sessions are chaired to keep the pace manageable, keep us on track, and key questions get discussed and written up. 
Be mindful of working in the shared language, speak slowly and clearly, and don't be shy - you can also ask people to slow down if needed.

**What if I can't attend at all now?**
That would be a huge shame for you to miss out, and for the group to miss you too, but we understand these things happen. Please tell Emma as soon as you can. 
You will still receive the synthesised updates from the workshop and an offer of a debrief call from Emma, but you would not get the same say in how the project progresses or work gets divided, hence it is important to make time for this, as progress made will be a core part of the project that you signed up to.

## Pre-work

**I am struggling to understand what requires my sign-up on LWE**
LWE has a few log ins/links to gain access to that all of us should be logged in and familiar with by now, but if not:
1. the main LWE organisation. (https://my.lifewatch.dev/user/me). They prefer you to be logged in with your main institutional email if possible.
2. the Modelling and Workflows Working Group. (https://community.lifewatch.eu/working-groups/modelling/). You have to request to join, but joining is quite quick. This gives you access to:
3. the LWE chat room*. (https://chat.lifewatch.dev/). This is our informal area for progressing the different workflows and chatting with one another, as well as the wider Working Group which includes participants and groups outside of the PREDICT project who are also working on shared model workflows for ecological forecasting applications.
4. GitLab. (https://gitlab.lifewatch.dev/). This is the medium for getting your code working, checked, versioned and eventually published in the LWE ecosystem.
5. The ICT help desk (https://ictdesk.lifewatch.eu/). This is to raise issues or requests regarding anything workflow related so the work can be assigned and shared fairly around the LWE team. 
The easiest way is to try and make sure you have the same credentials across all these sites to make logging in easier.

* the chat room is still very new, and in beta mode as an open-source/free version chat space, a bit like Slack. Just message Emma if you are having issues and she will liaise and feedback to LWE.

**What if I can't get the Docker part working?**
We will organise a pre-workshop online drop-in session, TBC early September, date and link to join via email.

**My institution/hardware limitations won't let me install Docker**
Start the IT request now to get it installed. If you are a case study leader (i.e., it is your project being integrated into LWE) you should have full autonomy over your workflows, and docker is an important element to learn, as we will all find out! 
If all else fails, LWE have offered to run the docker testing. This will need to be raised directly with them through their ticketing system: https://ictdesk.lifewatch.eu/
If you do not have an account for their ticketing system (this is separate to their main login page), email Emma and she will help get you set up.


**My workflow isn't in R. Does that matter?**
No, not at all. LWE uses docker to allow workflows to run seamlessly across languages. We just need to appropriately create your .json and .docker file with your language clearly defined.

**I thought we were sharing some workflow components between us?**
Correct, and we are. It is Emma's job to speak with you and tease out overlapping coding efforts and share across the group who is doing what. Copernicus data extractor (and future pulls from rchelsa) are in beta testing (GitLab development) as is Maria's forecasting skill component. Our workshop will help cement where the remaining efforts should go, and bring out into the open what everyone is working on and how it can be shared among the group.


**Do I really need to do all three pre-work parts before travelling?**
Yes, or tell us why you can't. The alternative is spending the technical day watching other people work and not progressing your own workflow which would be a waste of your time.

## Travel

**Where and when exactly?**
EBD-CSIC Red Building, Sevilla, from 10:00 on Wednesday 14th October 2026. There will be tea/coffee and toilet facilities while we congregate and wait for all of us to arrive.
Coach will leave from outside EBD at 11:00. Full details on [Travel and kit]({{ '/logistics/' | relative_url }}).

**What if my flight is delayed and I miss the coach?**
Always a possibility if flying same day. No worries, these things are out of our control. Just message Emma as soon as you know to let the group to go ahead without you. We will help you work out the route between Sevilla and , but you will need to make your own way to the site — about €100 by taxi. Keep the receipt and we can work out reimbursement later. Cheaper but less flexible alternative: Take a Bus (L-218 Pueblos) from Sevilla to El Rocío and we can arrange pick up from there into the wetland.
Sevilla bus station is: Estación de Autobuses Plaza de Armas - what3words (///wiggly.park.quality) / google (https://maps.app.goo.gl/btQzg91yba3DxWJD8). These only run a few times per day, so may not be so helpful.

**Should I arrive in Sevilla the night before?**
Most likely, unless you have an early incoming flight the day we depart to Doñana. Partners that have PREDICT workshop money can claim hotel expenses (for Tuesday 15th Oct) back via their budgets along with their flights. The money is already in each university's accounts. Please speak to Emma/Maria if you have more questions on that. We will be covering the cost of accommodation for the main workshop days.

**When should I book my flight home?**
Nothing departing before 19:30 on Friday 16th October 2026. The coach reaches Sevilla around 16:30 and traffic is unpredictable.

**Can I bring a partner or family?**
Accommodation and catering are booked for registered participants only and the site has limited capacity. Family are welcome to stay in nearby Mataslascañas. It is not possible to walk through the park on foot. Separate travel options to get you on/off site will need to be arranged.

**Can I drive all or part the way instead?**
You are welcome to drive to the Sevilla meeting point and park for free in the outdoor parking lot behind our office buildings (use what3words: [///sings.enacted.quantity]) / google: [https://maps.app.goo.gl/3wXWp6yarsVtgW52A]
It is preferred if we all arrive together by coach into the park to easily count people in and out at the gatehouse. Personal vehicles inside the park boundary might not be permitted, but speak to us and make us aware of your plans by filling out the travel/arrival form: [https://ee.kobotoolbox.org/x/vMj7Shfl] and can overcome most things together with a little notice.

## Staying there

**Am I sharing a room?**
Most likely yes with one other person, due to limited space of the venue. We will arrange rooms by gender.

**Are towels provided?**
Yes, towels and bedding linen are provided.

**Is there a shop nearby?**
Assume no (nearest is Matalascañas ~20-30 mins through and outside of the park by car). You cannot reach the town directly on foot from the park (the boundary is fenced).
Bring anything you cannot do without e.g., specific medications, contact lens solution, the usual toiletries, any snacks you might want.

**Will there be Wi-Fi?**
WiFi will be available, but maybe not super reliable. Do your heavy downloads before you travel. :)

## Money

**What's covered by us?** 
Accommodation, all meals at Doñana itself, and the coach ride both ways. 
We aim to hire a catering team to provide us with lunch and dinner each afternoon/evening (we are requesting your dietary requirements for that here: [https://ee.kobotoolbox.org/x/8be7qdKo]).
Breakfast will be sourced by Maria and Emma, but will be a 'DIY' / at your own pace each morning. We will make sure the essentials are there: tea, coffee, juice, bread, eggs etc. and will ask you for anything specific closer to the date.

**What is covered by the PREDICT budget already allocated to specific institutions?** 
Your travel to and from Sevilla, accommodation for the night before (Tuesday 13th October) in Sevilla if required.

**What is not covered?**
Personal spending. Additional snacks.

**How do I claim travel back?**
Through your own or a shared partner institution depending on specific partner agreements set up by the PREDICT budget. Keep original receipts and tickets / boarding passes. Speak to Emma/Maria for more information.

## Everything else

**Is there a code of conduct?**
Yes — treat each other with kindness, compassion and respect. Please share any concerns with Emma, and if that is not appropriate, Maria.

**Will there be photos?**
Yes, for project dissemination. Speak to Emma if you would rather not appear in any and we will make sure of it. Nothing goes online without your consent.

**When will I get the outputs?**
A written synthesis within 10 working days with decisions, actions, roadmap, and every wall photographed. See [How we work]({{ '/working-practices/' | relative_url }}).

To be agreed by the end of Day 3.
