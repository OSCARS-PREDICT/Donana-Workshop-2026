## Where we are staying in Doñana

**Reserva Biológica de Doñana (RBD) / Doñana Biological Reserve (RBD)** See [our location](https://what3words.com/heroics.documenting.positivity) inside Parque Nacional de Doñana / National Park of Doñana, which covers a large area of wetland (6,794 Ha). This is state-owned and managed by EBD-CSIC. EBD-CSIC coordinates research inside this area, and this part is non-public. Our workshop is a great opportunity to explore this beautiful habitat and wildlife. 
The wider Doñana Natural Area (National Park and Natural Park since 1978) is over 120,000 Ha.
[See more info about the Park here](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/en/Organismos-y-Centros/ICTS/CienciasMar/RBD.html)

## What to bring

October in the Doñana area means warm / hot days with an average of ~20°C (10 - 30°C) with roughly 11 hrs of daylight, cooler mornings and evenings, strong sun, and a chance of wind (~4m/s) or rain (~2mm/day). Averages taken from [moriatlas](https://www.moriatlas.com/en/park/donana-national-park/climate).

### Essential

<ul class="check">
<li><strong>Laptop and charger</strong> — with all the pre-requisites completed ahead of the workshop (see prework)</li>
<li><strong>EU plug adapter</strong> — Spain uses Type F/C, 230 V.</li>
<li><strong>Comfortable closed walking shoes or light boots</strong> — trails are sandy, uneven, sometimes muddy. Sandals not recommended for the field sessions</li>
<li><strong>Layers</strong> — a t-shirt at midday and a fleece at 08:00 on the same day is normal</li>
<li><strong>Light waterproof or windproof jacket</strong></li>
<li><strong>Sun hat, sunglasses, high-factor sunscreen</strong> — open wetland offers no shade when sunny</li>
<li><strong>Insect repellent</strong></li>
<li><strong>Refillable water bottle</strong> — refill points on site</li>
<li><strong>Personal medication</strong> in sufficient quantity, plus the everyday things: antihistamines, painkillers, plasters, we will also provide first aid kits</li>
<li><strong>EHIC / GHIC and travel insurance details</strong></li>
</ul>

### Worth bringing

<ul class="check">
<li>Binoculars if you own them, but we will have some spare pairs or happy to share too.</li>
<li>Power bank — sockets in the meeting room will be scarce</li>
<li>Head torch or a phone with a good torch — the site is genuinely dark and paths are unlit</li>
<li>Decent footwear, walking boots, walking shoes, trail shoes, or sports shoes</li>
<li>Long sleeves and trousers for dawn and dusk — the best birding hours are also the best mosquito hours</li>
<li>Something to change into for dinner. Definitely nothing formal, but you may want to be out of field clothes and into something comfier</li>
<li>Small daypack for walks</li>
<li>Standard toiletries</li>
<li>Earplugs for light sleepers</li>
<li>Muted colours, if you would rather see birds than the backs of birds</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

Walks will take place on relatively flat sandy tracks at conversational pace. **Every walking session has a seated indoor alternative running in parallel for those that prefer.** 
The venue has kitchen for us to use, shared bathrooms, dining/lounging area and shared twin-rooms, the accommodation is lovely, but basic, manage expectations, it is a field station.

If you have any mobility, sensory, dietary, medical or other access requirement, email [Emma](mailto:emma.underwood@ebd.csic.es) by <span class="chip chip--quiet">Monday 31st August 2026</span>. We would much rather adjust the programme and accommodate requests than have you struggle quietly.
